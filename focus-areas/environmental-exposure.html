Focus area

Everyday Air Exposure

Dust, smoke, cooking fumes and seasonal pollen all reach your airways. The formula includes stinging nettle and quercetin, two ingredients often used in seasonal-wellness products.

Stinging nettle

Nettle has a long tradition in European herbal practice. The National Cancer Institute's dictionary describes its extract as having potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity, which comes mainly from preclinical research.

Your environment comes first

EPA guidance names pollutant sources and poor ventilation as the main causes of indoor air problems. Cutting down on smoke and fumes and ventilating well are the most effective steps you can take.

Where a supplement fits

Think of Pulmo Balance as one small part of a wider plan that starts with cleaner air, not as protection against pollution.
