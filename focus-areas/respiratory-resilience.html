Related ingredients

What Pulmo Balance Includes for Long-Term Respiratory Resilience

Tiger Milk Mushroom

Lignosus rhinocerus

Tiger milk mushroom has traditional use in Southeast Asia for coughs and chest comfort. The seller includes it to promote lung tissue vitality and long-term respiratory health. A 2021 study of 50 volunteers reported positive changes, but it had no placebo group, so the evidence is preliminary.

BioPerine®

Black pepper extract

BioPerine® is a black pepper extract standardized for piperine. The seller names it as the seventh ingredient, and piperine is often paired with botanical extracts in supplements.

Research on individual ingredients does not prove the finished product works. See the references.
