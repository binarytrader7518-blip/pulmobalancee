Seasonal & Nasal Comfort

Why stinging nettle and quercetin are included for upper-airway and nasal comfort as the seasons change.

Why This Area Matters

When the seasons turn, pollen, dry heating, cold air and humidity swings can all change how your nose and upper airways feel. Seasonal comfort is about preparing your routine and environment for those shifts.

Related ingredients

What Pulmo Balance Includes for Seasonal

Stinging Nettle Extract

Urtica dioica

Stinging nettle is a leafy plant with a long history in seasonal routines. The seller includes it to help keep upper respiratory and nasal passages clear. Small human trials on nettle and seasonal nasal symptoms show mixed results, with improvement often seen in placebo groups too.

Quercetin Dihydrate

Plant flavonoid

Quercetin is a flavonoid found in onions, apples, berries and tea. The seller includes it for a healthy histamine response during seasonal changes. Most quercetin research is from laboratory and animal studies.

Research on individual ingredients does not prove the finished product works. See the references.

Everyday habits

Habits That Support This Area

  • Check local pollen forecasts and keep windows closed on high-pollen days.
  • Shower and change clothes after time outdoors in pollen season.
  • Wash bedding weekly in hot water.
  • Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about seasonal symptoms that disrupt sleep or work.
