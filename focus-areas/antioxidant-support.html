Antioxidant Support

Every breath brings oxygen in and creates by-products that the body's antioxidant systems manage. Two ingredients bring plant antioxidants to the formula.

Maritime pine bark

Pine bark extract is rich in procyanidins. MedlinePlus rates one specific extract, Pycnogenol, as possibly effective for asthma symptoms when taken alongside asthma medicine. The seller doesn't say which pine bark extract Pulmo Balance uses, so those findings may not carry over.

Quercetin

Quercetin is a flavonoid found in onions, apples, berries and tea. Reviews describe strong antioxidant activity in laboratory studies, while human results are mixed.

Food first

A colourful diet of fruit and vegetables is the most reliable source of antioxidants. A supplement can add to it but can't replace it.
