Pulmo Balance Review

Our verdict up front: Pulmo Balance is a reasonably priced botanical formula with a sensible ingredient list and a generous 90-day guarantee. It falls short on dosage transparency, and the finished formula hasn't been clinically tested.

The short version

If you want a simple daily botanical routine for breathing comfort and you're happy to commit for a few months, Pulmo Balance is a reasonable option. Buy the 3 or 6-bottle pack for the best price per bottle and free US shipping. If you need treatment for a lung condition, a supplement isn't the answer; see your doctor.

What we like

  • A thoughtful combination of traditional botanicals, plant flavonoids, an enzyme and a medicinal mushroom
  • Simple directions: two capsules with a meal
  • A 90-day money-back guarantee, longer than many supplements offer
  • Bundle pricing down to $49 a bottle, with free US shipping

What could be better

  • Amounts per serving aren't published
  • No clinical trials of the finished product
  • Refunds exclude shipping and return postage
  • Only available online
