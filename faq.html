FAQ

Pulmo Balance Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about ingredients, directions, pricing, shipping and the 90-day guarantee, based on the seller's published information.

What is Pulmo Balance?

Pulmo Balance is a daily dietary supplement in capsule form, sold as "Daily Lung Support · Advanced Respiratory Formula." It combines seven named ingredients: mullein, bromelain, maritime pine bark, stinging nettle, tiger milk mushroom, quercetin and BioPerine®. The seller positions it to support healthy respiratory function and natural airway clearance. Learn more on our product overview.

What ingredients are in Pulmo Balance?

The seller lists Mullein Extract, Bromelain Powder, Maritime Pine Bark, Stinging Nettle Extract, Tiger Milk Mushroom and Quercetin Dihydrate Extract, and names BioPerine® (black pepper extract) as the seventh ingredient. See what each one is included for on the ingredients section.

Does the seller list the amount of each ingredient?

The seller's pages we reviewed name the ingredients but do not publish per-ingredient amounts or a full Supplement Facts panel. Check the Supplement Facts label on the bottle when it arrives, and share it with your doctor if you take other medicines or supplements.

How do I take Pulmo Balance?

The seller's directions are two capsules a day for adults, taken with a meal and 6–8 oz of water, or as directed by a healthcare professional. Many people split them between breakfast and dinner. See our How It Works page for a simple routine.

How long does one bottle last?

Each bottle contains 60 capsules. At the recommended two capsules a day, one bottle is a 30-day supply. The 3-bottle pack covers 90 days and the 6-bottle pack covers 180 days.

How long until I notice a difference?

Everyone is different, and there is no guaranteed timeline. Supplements tend to be used for weeks or months as part of a consistent routine, which is why the seller's packages are sized at 60, 90 and 180 days. The 90-day guarantee gives you time to decide.

Who should not take Pulmo Balance?

The seller says the product is not for anyone under 18 or for women who are pregnant or nursing. If you take prescription medication, use an inhaler, or manage any health condition, talk with your doctor first. Pulmo Balance does not replace prescribed treatment.

Can Pulmo Balance treat asthma, COPD or other lung conditions?

No. Pulmo Balance is a dietary supplement. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for inhalers, medication or medical care. If you have breathing difficulties, see a healthcare professional.

Is shipping free?

Shipping is free within the US on the 3-bottle and 6-bottle packages. The 2-bottle package adds a shipping charge, which the seller shows at checkout.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. The seller offers a 90-day money-back guarantee. Under the seller's terms, products returned within 90 days, including empty bottles, are refunded minus shipping and handling fees. Contact the seller's customer support to start a return.

What bonuses come with Pulmo Balance?

The 3-bottle and 6-bottle packages include two digital guides as instant downloads: The 7-Day Lung Reset Protocol and The Inflammation-Free Breathing Blueprint. The 2-bottle package does not include bonuses.

Where is Pulmo Balance made?
