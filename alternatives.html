Pulmo Balance Alternatives

Other ways to approach respiratory wellness, from single-ingredient supplements to lifestyle changes, and when to see a professional instead.

Single-ingredient supplements

Bromelain, quercetin and pine bark extract are all sold on their own with published doses. That makes it easier to know exactly what you're taking, but you'll need several products to cover the same ground.

Food-first approach

Onions, apples, berries and tea provide quercetin and other flavonoids, and pineapple contains bromelain in small amounts.

Cleaner air at home

EPA guidance on reducing pollutant sources and improving ventilation is one of the most effective things you can do. Read our guide.

Medical advice

Persistent cough, wheezing or shortness of breath call for a doctor, not a supplement. Ask your doctor about lung function tests if you're concerned.
