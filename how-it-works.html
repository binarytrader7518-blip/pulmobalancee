How it works

How Pulmo Balance Works

How the formula is put together, how to take it, and how it fits alongside the daily habits that matter most for healthy breathing.

The approach

A Multi-Ingredient Approach

Rather than relying on one herb, Pulmo Balance brings together ingredients that play different roles. Keep in mind that each ingredient has been researched on its own, not as part of this formula.

Traditional throat & chest herb

Mullein brings the botanical, traditional-use side of the formula.

Protein-digesting enzyme

Bromelain is an enzyme complex that is often paired with flavonoids such as quercetin.

Polyphenol source

Pine bark supplies procyanidins, which are studied for antioxidant activity.

Plant flavonoid

Quercetin is one of the most studied dietary flavonoids.

Medicinal mushroom

Tiger milk mushroom has traditional use for respiratory complaints in Southeast Asia.

Absorption helper

BioPerine is included to help the body absorb the other ingredients.

Directions

How to Take Pulmo Balance

1

Two capsules with a meal

This is the seller's recommended serving. Take it with a glass of water and don't exceed it.

2

Once a day, every day

Choose a meal you never skip, such as breakfast, so it becomes automatic.

3

Commit to the timeline

The seller recommends at least four months of consistent use. Track how you feel each month.

Check first: it is not intended for under-18s or for women who are pregnant or nursing. Ask your doctor or pharmacist first if you take prescription medicine, as bromelain and piperine can interact with some drugs.

Your routine

A Sensible Daily Routine

Supplements are designed to complement healthy habits, not replace them.

Morning

Take your two capsules with breakfast. Open a window for a few minutes if the outdoor air is good.

Daytime

Get some movement in, such as a brisk walk, and keep a water bottle nearby.

Evening

Use your kitchen extractor fan when cooking and keep smoke out of the house.

Night

Aim for consistent, good-quality sleep in a well-ventilated bedroom.
