How it works

How Pulmo Balance Fits Into Your Day

A plain-language look at the formula's approach, the seller's directions and a realistic daily routine, so you know exactly what to expect.

The approach

Three Layers in One Formula

The seller groups the ingredients into roles. Here's how they fit together.

Botanical base

Mullein, stinging nettle and tiger milk mushroom have traditional use in respiratory and seasonal routines. They form the plant foundation of the blend.

Enzyme & antioxidants

Bromelain is an enzyme from pineapple. Maritime pine bark and quercetin are plant antioxidants included for everyday cellular defense.

Absorption partner

BioPerine® is a black pepper extract. Piperine is commonly added to botanical formulas; the seller names it as the seventh ingredient.

These roles describe the seller's formulation approach. They have not been tested together in a published trial of Pulmo Balance.

Directions

How to Take Pulmo Balance

The seller's directions for adults: take two capsules daily with a meal and 6–8 oz of water, or as directed by a healthcare professional.

Morning capsule

Take one capsule with breakfast and a full glass of water.

Evening capsule

Take the second capsule with dinner. Prefer one sitting? Take both with your main meal.

Repeat daily

Keep the bottle somewhere you'll see it. Store it closed in a cool, dry place.

Don't take more than the label serving. Not for anyone under 18, or for pregnant or nursing women. Ask your doctor first if you take medication; bromelain and quercetin, for example, can interact with some medicines.

What to expect

Setting Realistic Expectations

Supplements are not fast-acting. People typically use a formula like this for several weeks as part of a steady routine, which is why the seller offers 60-, 90- and 180-day packages. Results vary from person to person, and some people notice little change.

The seller's 90-day guarantee gives you a window to decide. If you have a persistent cough, wheezing, chest pain or shortness of breath, see a doctor rather than waiting on a supplement.
