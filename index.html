What Is Pulmo Balance?

Pulmo Balance is a dietary supplement sold as Daily Lung Support · Advanced Respiratory Formula. Each bottle holds 60 capsules, and the routine is simple: two capsules a day with food and a glass of water.

The formula brings together seven ingredients named by the seller: mullein extract, bromelain powder, maritime pine bark, stinging nettle extract, tiger milk mushroom, quercetin dihydrate and BioPerine®. It is designed for adults who want to give their airways everyday support alongside healthy habits, not as a replacement for medical care.

Inside the formula

The Seven Pulmo Balance Ingredients

Every ingredient below is named by the seller. Descriptions explain why the seller includes it; they are not proof of results.

Mullein Extract

Verbascum thapsus

A soft-leaved herb with a long history in traditional respiratory routines. The seller includes it for natural mucus clearance and a soothed respiratory tract.

Bromelain Powder

Pineapple-stem enzyme

A protein-digesting enzyme taken from pineapple. The seller says it is included to help maintain a balanced inflammatory response in the bronchial tubes.

Maritime Pine Bark

Pinus pinaster

A bark extract rich in proanthocyanidins, a family of plant antioxidants. The seller includes it for antioxidant support and healthy circulation in the lungs.

Stinging Nettle Extract

Urtica dioica

A leafy plant used in seasonal routines for generations. The seller includes it to help keep upper respiratory and nasal passages clear.

Tiger Milk Mushroom

Lignosus rhinocerus

A Southeast Asian mushroom traditionally used for coughs and chest comfort. The seller includes it for lung tissue vitality and long-term respiratory health.

Quercetin Dihydrate

Plant flavonoid

A flavonoid naturally found in foods such as onions and apples. The seller includes it for a healthy histamine response during seasonal changes.

BioPerine®

Black pepper extract

A piperine extract from black pepper, named by the seller among the formula's seven ingredients. Piperine is commonly paired with botanicals in supplements.

Pulmo Balance Questions, Answered

What is Pulmo Balance?

Pulmo Balance is a daily dietary supplement in capsule form, sold as "Daily Lung Support · Advanced Respiratory Formula." It combines seven named ingredients: mullein, bromelain, maritime pine bark, stinging nettle, tiger milk mushroom, quercetin and BioPerine®. The seller positions it to support healthy respiratory function and natural airway clearance. Learn more on our product overview.

What ingredients are in Pulmo Balance?

The seller lists Mullein Extract, Bromelain Powder, Maritime Pine Bark, Stinging Nettle Extract, Tiger Milk Mushroom and Quercetin Dihydrate Extract, and names BioPerine® (black pepper extract) as the seventh ingredient. See what each one is included for on the ingredients section.

How do I take Pulmo Balance?

The seller's directions are two capsules a day for adults, taken with a meal and 6–8 oz of water, or as directed by a healthcare professional. Many people split them between breakfast and dinner. See our How It Works page for a simple routine.

How long does one bottle last?

Each bottle contains 60 capsules. At the recommended two capsules a day, one bottle is a 30-day supply. The 3-bottle pack covers 90 days and the 6-bottle pack covers 180 days.

Who should not take Pulmo Balance?

The seller says the product is not for anyone under 18 or for women who are pregnant or nursing. If you take prescription medication, use an inhaler, or manage any health condition, talk with your doctor first. Pulmo Balance does not replace prescribed treatment.
