Six Ways Pulmo Balance Is Designed to Help

Mullein

Natural airway clearance

The seller includes mullein to support natural mucus clearance and a soothed respiratory tract. Mullein has centuries of traditional use, though modern human trials are limited.

Scientific References & Ingredient Research

Explore government health resources and published research related to the ingredients and topics discussed on this website.

NIH · National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health

Bromelain: Usefulness and Safety

NCCIH summarizes the limited research on oral bromelain, notes that high-quality evidence for sinus symptoms is lacking, and covers side effects and interactions.

US Government Health ResourceBromelainView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (MedlinePlus)

Maritime Pine

MedlinePlus reviews what is known about maritime pine bark extract, including evidence ratings for different uses and safety information.

US Government Health ResourceMaritime Pine BarkView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed)

Common mullein (Verbascum thapsus L.): recent advances in research

A 2005 review in Phytotherapy Research of mullein's traditional respiratory uses and laboratory findings. It is a literature review, not a clinical trial.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchMulleinView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed)

Health-promoting and disease-mitigating potential of Verbascum thapsus L. (common mullein): A review

A 2022 review summarizing mullein's plant compounds and reported biological activities, much of it from laboratory and animal studies.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchMulleinView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed)

Effect of tiger milk mushroom (Lignosus rhinocerus) supplementation on respiratory health, immunity and antioxidant status: an open-label prospective study

A 2021 study in Scientific Reports where 50 volunteers took tiger milk mushroom for three months. It was open-label with no placebo group, so results are preliminary.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchTiger Milk MushroomView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed Central)

A Status Review of the Bioactive Activities of Tiger Milk Mushroom Lignosus rhinocerotis (Cooke) Ryvarden

A 2018 review in Frontiers in Pharmacology of the mushroom's traditional use for coughs and asthma and of laboratory and animal findings.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchTiger Milk MushroomView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed)

Efficacy of Supportive Therapy of Allergic Rhinitis by Stinging Nettle (Urtica dioica) root extract: a Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Clinical Trial

A small 2017 human trial on nettle root extract in people with seasonal nasal symptoms. Symptom scores improved in both the nettle and placebo groups.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchStinging NettleView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed)

Randomized, double-blind study of freeze-dried Urtica dioica in the treatment of allergic rhinitis

A 1990 one-week trial of freeze-dried nettle. It was rated slightly better than placebo overall, with only small differences in symptom diaries.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchStinging NettleView Official Research NIH · National Library of Medicine (PubMed Central)

Pharmacological Activity of Quercetin: An Updated Review

A 2022 review of quercetin's antioxidant and other reported activities, drawing largely on laboratory and animal research.

Peer-Reviewed ResearchQuercetinView Official Research NIH · National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute

How the Lungs Work

NHLBI explains how the lungs exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide, how breathing is controlled, and habits that help protect lung health.

US Government Health ResourceLung health basicsView Official Research NIH · National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health

Using Dietary Supplements Wisely

NCCIH guidance on how supplement evidence varies, possible medication interactions, and why supplements are regulated differently from drugs.
